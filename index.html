Guillermo Kulemeyer

Physicist and PhD student in Computational Intelligence

I develop deep-learning models for scientific problems involving structure and uncertainty. My current research focuses on predictive and generative models for RNA structure.

I study how these models learn structural representations, generalize to less familiar sequences, and represent multiple plausible solutions.
